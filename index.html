DECOR DESIGNS · JUNGLE LUDO

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 19 October 2026

Jungle Ludo is an offline board game from Decor Designs. This policy describes the advertising-supported version 1.0.0 of the Android application, package com.jungleludo.app.

Information on your phone

The game stores player names you enter, selected characters and team colors, match progress, language, sound and vibration preferences locally in the app's private storage. These details are used to run and resume your match. They are not sent to Decor Designs or other parties by the app.

Advertising and information processed by Google

Google AdMob supplies a labeled banner on the home screen. No ads appear on the game board. We apply child age treatment to all players, limit ad content to G, disable publisher personalization and first-party ID, request non-personalized advertising, and remove Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox ad permissions. We do not send player names, characters or saved matches to the ad service.

Advertising still involves data processing. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address (including approximate location inferred from IP), ad interactions, diagnostics, and permitted device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Child treatment restricts this processing; it does not mean advertising is completely data-free. SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google handles its own retention and data controls under its policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for advertising.

Google's User Messaging Platform checks the applicable privacy status. Ads are requested only when its permission check allows them. If privacy options are required, they are available in Settings & privacy. If the privacy check or network is unavailable, the game remains usable without advertising. Gameplay itself works offline. We do not include Firebase Analytics, mediation networks, accounts, chat, online multiplayer or purchases.

Retention and deletion

Your saved match stays on the device until you replace it, delete it in Settings & privacy, clear the app's storage through Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting the saved match removes its player names and progress but keeps your settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local data. Cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for game data.

Children and families

The same game-storage practices and the advertising protections described above apply to every player. The app does not ask for a date of birth, contact details or identifying documents. All players receive the same child-directed advertising treatment. We do not use an age gate to relax child protections for adults. The game does not ask children to create accounts or share their names with other players online.

Contacting support

If you choose to email support, your email provider transmits your email address and message. We use the information you provide to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information in support messages.

Changes to this policy

If future versions change data handling or introduce online features, this policy and any required in-app notices will be updated before those features are released. The practices above describe the stated version only.
